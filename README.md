This is an Independant Study between Corbin Wright and Fanchao Meng for the 2026 fall semester at Misericordia University

The goal is to take my experience with dynamic system and control theory and apply this to my ambition with the field of Physical Therapy. The project is to create a digital twin of the human musculoskeletal system that can allow us to tune different parameters to reflect different conditions you may see in the real world. This tool would have benfits within the clinics and students preparing for their time in the field. This project will be just laying the groundwork for a much more in depth product which will be highly customizable and reflects all the major systems that impact movement.
