Independent Study · LB-AO musculoskeletal digital twin · Phase D, Stages D1–D3

A squat, built from an ontology of the human body

This page shows what the project has built so far. It is a side-view squat for three body types, driven by 14 leg muscles. Every number traces back to a published source or is labelled as an assumption. Two AI models built and checked it, and Corbin decided and merged each step. The computation is verified, but it has not yet been compared with measured people.

01 · THE SIMULATION

Watch the squat, muscle by muscle

The squat is 1.5 s down and 1.5 s up, with the foot flat. At each instant the model does two things. It works out the joint torques the motion needs (inverse dynamics), then shares those torques among the 14 muscles using the least total effort. Muscles are coloured by how hard they work.

02 · INGREDIENTS

What went into it

The project keeps anatomy, numbers and models apart. The ontology says what exists and how it connects. The datasets hold the numbers, each tied to a source. The model only uses data that passes a traceability check, and every run writes a manifest recording exactly which files it used.

        06 · THE BIGGER PICTURE

        How the squat serves the project

        The long-term aim is a digital twin of the human musculoskeletal system. Its parameters could be tuned to reflect conditions seen in physical therapy, for use in clinics and by students. That needs groundwork first. The squat is a proof of concept for two claims the larger project rests on.

        CLAIM A

        Working with AI can produce legitimate scientific research

          CLAIM B

          An ontology of the musculoskeletal system can build a digital twin

            How the work is done

            Corbin decides and merges. One AI model builds, and the other reviews with AGREE, AMEND or DISAGREE. They swap roles under the same one-page role rules. Every decision, its defence and how disagreements were settled go into a log that collaborators can audit. Developing this way of working is half the project's value.

            Where it goes next

            Stages D1–D3 are done. The next steps are a plan still awaiting Corbin's decisions, not a commitment. Conditions and personalization come in the larger project.
