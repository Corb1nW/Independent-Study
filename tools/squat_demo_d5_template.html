Stage D5: every lower-limb muscle in the ontology, all 43 leg muscles in the squat

D4 showed that a muscle with sourced anatomy enters the squat as data. D5 does it at scale. Every lower-limb muscle named in the StatPearls hip, thigh, leg and foot chapters is now in the ontology, on both sides, with its crossings, nerves, actions and evidence. That includes the muscles no model can drive yet. Gait2392's 27 remaining leg muscles entered the squat in commits that changed no code, giving 43 actuators against D4's 16. The physics is unchanged, so every difference in the results comes from the anatomy alone. Everything below is verified, meaning computed correctly. None of it is yet validated against measured people. Status: the build is awaiting ChatGPT's review (PR #55), so nothing on this page is accepted yet.

01 · THE SQUAT WITH 43 MUSCLES

The same squat, now driven by every Gait2392 leg muscle

The motion is the designed D1 squat: 1.5 s down, 1.5 s up, foot flat, moving knee. 03 · EVERY LOWER-LIMB MUSCLE, PLACED

The anatomy inventory: what the model can and cannot drive

Corbin's scope (Q80) was every muscle in the lower half of the body, "even if they don't offer much contribution to our simulated movement currently". The C5 register places each muscle entity in exactly one group, generated from the ontology on every run. Muscles the model cannot drive are recorded, not dropped, so later stages (foot joints, out-of-plane motion, the left leg) have a sourced inventory to build on.

04 · THE ONTOLOGY AND THE MODEL CHECK EACH OTHER

Every direction disagreement declared, with its basis, and confirmed by OpenSim

Many of the new muscles act mainly out of the sagittal plane, and Gait2392's paths give them sagittal moment arms the sources do not list. D-063 Amendment 1 (ChatGPT's D5-R01) bounds how that is allowed. A disagreement of at least 1 mm passes only if it is declared in the specification (actuator, coordinate, knee variant, direction, basis) and OpenSim's own geometry, read without any ontology capability, has a moment arm of at least 1 mm with the same sign at that sample. An undeclared, unconfirmed or stale declaration fails. No capability was added without a source.

A5: the D4 runs, regenerated

Both official D4 configurations were rerun at 4c0e320 and compared with their af21e78 manifests by ci/d4-verify/compare_manifest.py. Every series, status, regression result, oracle comparison and H1 value is equal. The differences are confined to what D-063 Amendment 1 allows: the new C3 fields (the declared lateral gastrocnemius deviation and the policy name), the C5 inventory, input hashes, the commit, and the second-solver residuals that F1 changed. One point is recorded for the reviewer. At the build head, D4's older C5 list of muscles that cross a modelled joint without an actuator grows from 15 to 26, because steps 2–4 added leg muscles to the shared ontology. At step 1 alone, before any anatomy, the C5 sections differed only in the new split. hip.yaml still loads on its own.

      09 · LIMITS

      What D5 does not show

      Planar model, out-of-plane muscles

      Gluteus medius and minimus, TFL, piriformis, the gemelli and quadratus femoris mainly abduct or rotate the hip. In a sagittal model their paths can still supply flexion or extension, without the frontal-plane balance that limits them in a real squat. Their share is reported, of the hip moment, rather than hidden. It is an artifact of the model, not a finding about people.
